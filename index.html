2. In your project: Settings → API → copy the Project URL and anon/public key
3. Paste them below and click Connect. Your CRM data will sync to Supabase from that point forward.

Team Members & Roles

How team access works: Invite people via Supabase Dashboard → Authentication → Users → Invite User. Once they sign in, they appear here. New users default to Staff role. Promote to Admin to grant full access (Settings, Approvals, Delete, etc.).

Adding here pre-assigns their role. They still need to be invited via Supabase Dashboard and complete their sign-up to actually log in.

Counter Management

Reset the Total Raised counter on the dashboard to start fresh from a specific date (e.g. start of a new fiscal year). Your historical donation data stays intact — only the visible counter changes. You can clear the baseline anytime to show all-time totals again.
